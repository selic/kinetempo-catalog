Frequently asked

The lock-screen widget stops changing colour

iOS limits how often an app in the background may update a Live Activity. The countdown keeps running natively and the widget shows the next steps; sounds are unaffected. Unlock the phone or open the app to refresh it.

No sound with the silent switch on

Kinetempo uses the playback audio category, so cues play even in silent mode. Check the media volume and that Sounds is enabled in Settings.

How do I share a program with a patient or a friend?

Open the program → Share. Send the link, show the QR code, or export a file. Local videos are not included in shares; use links for videos.

How do clinics publish programs?

See the authoring guide: create a publisher folder, add program files, open a pull request.
